# Batch 3 scripts: engagement and retention features

Continues [Batch 2](./2026-08-03-batch-2-setup-scripts.md). Same format:
vertical 9:16, 25 to 45 seconds, screen recording + voiceover, cold open on a
pain point, plain concrete English, no hype, no emoji, no invented claims.

---

## 17. Message your landlord or tenant directly

**Role:** Landlord/agent/tenant · **Length:** ~20s

**Hook:** "Stop switching to a separate messaging app to talk about a repair
or a payment."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: tenant/lease page, tap "Message." VO: "From the tenant's profile,
   tap Message." Caption: "Step 1: open the chat"
2. Screen: send a text message. VO: "Send a message, it's real time, and
   they'll get a notification." Caption: "Step 2: send"
3. Screen: reply appears. VO: "Everything about that lease, in one thread,
   attached to the right property."

**End card:** "Have a quick question instead? Ask the assistant." (leads into
video 18)

## 18. Ask the AI assistant a question

**Role:** Landlord/agent/tenant · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook:** "Instead of digging through reports, just ask: how much rent came in
this month?"

**Beats:**
1. Screen: open Assistant tab. VO: "Open the Assistant from your menu."
   Caption: "Step 1: open the assistant"
2. Screen: type a question ("How much rent did I collect this month?"). VO:
   "Ask it in plain language, like how much rent you collected this month."
   Caption: "Step 2: ask a question"
3. Screen: assistant replies with the actual number/summary. VO: "It pulls the
   real answer from your account, right there in the chat."

**End card:** "Here's how to stay on top of everything without opening the
app." (leads into video 19)

## 25. Split a shared bill with the building

**Role:** Landlord/tenant · **Length:** ~35s

**Hook:** "Water, fuel, security, cleaning: instead of chasing everyone
individually, split it once and let the app track who's paid."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: Community, "Create Shared Bill." VO: "From Community, tap Create
   Shared Bill." Caption: "Step 1: start a shared bill"
2. Screen: enter amount, choose split (equal / by unit / custom). VO: "Enter
   the total, and choose how to split it, equally, by unit, or a custom
   amount per tenant." Caption: "Step 2: choose the split"
3. Screen: published to the building, tenants see their share. VO: "Everyone
   in the building sees exactly what they owe." Caption: "Everyone sees their
   share"
4. Screen: tenant marks their share paid, others confirm. VO: "Tenants mark
   their share paid, and neighbors can confirm or flag a dispute."

**End card:** "For pooled funds, there's a shared wallet with a group
approval step. Here's how that works." (leads into video 26)

## 26. Use the community wallet and approve a payout

**Role:** Landlord/tenant · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook:** "Pooled money for something like security or repairs needs everyone
to agree before it's spent. Here's how that approval works."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: Community wallet, balance + dedicated account number. VO: "The
   community has its own wallet, with its own account number for topping up."
   Caption: "A shared wallet for the building"
2. Screen: someone requests a withdrawal. VO: "When someone requests a
   withdrawal," Caption: "Step 1: request a withdrawal"
3. Screen: other tenants vote/approve. VO: "the rest of the building votes to
   approve it before the money moves." Caption: "Step 2: the building votes"
4. Screen: approved, funds released. VO: "Once approved, the funds go out."

**End card:** "Next batch: the smaller things, still worth knowing." (series
bumper)
