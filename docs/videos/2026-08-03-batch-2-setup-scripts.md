# Batch 2 scripts: setup and onboarding features

Continues [Batch 1](./2026-08-03-batch-1-core-loop-scripts.md). Same format:
vertical 9:16, 25 to 45 seconds, screen recording + voiceover, cold open on a
pain point, plain concrete English, no hype, no emoji, no invented claims.

---

## 9. Verify your identity (KYC)

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook:** "Getting paid out to your bank account needs one thing first: proving
you are who you say you are."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: Profile, tap "Verify identity." VO: "Go to your profile and tap
   Verify Identity." Caption: "Step 1: start verification"
2. Screen: selfie capture. VO: "Take a quick selfie." Caption: "Step 2: selfie"
3. Screen: ID upload (NIN, driver's license, passport, or voter's card). VO:
   "Upload your NIN, driver's license, passport, or voter's card." Caption:
   "Step 3: upload your ID"
4. Screen: submitted, pending review state. VO: "Submit, and our team reviews
   it. You'll be notified once it's approved."

**End card:** "Verified? Add your bank account next." (leads into video 10)

---

## 10. Add your bank account

**Role:** Landlord · **Length:** ~25s

**Hook:** "Before your first payout, tell us where the money should go."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: Wallet, tap "Bank Accounts." VO: "From your wallet, open Bank
   Accounts." Caption: "Step 1: open Bank Accounts"
2. Screen: add account, enter bank + account number. VO: "Enter your bank and
   account number." Caption: "Step 2: enter your details"
3. Screen: verified account name shown, set as primary. VO: "We confirm the
   account name automatically. Set it as your primary account." Caption:
   "Step 3: set as primary"

**End card:** "Now here's how to withdraw your balance." (leads into video 11)

---

## 11. Check your wallet and request a payout

**Role:** Landlord · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook:** "Rent's been coming in. Here's how to move it to your bank account."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: Wallet screen, balance shown. VO: "Your wallet shows every payment
   that's come in." Caption: "Your balance, always visible"
2. Screen: tap "Withdraw," enter amount. VO: "Tap Withdraw and enter the
   amount." Caption: "Step 1: enter an amount"
3. Screen: confirm, payout processing. VO: "Confirm, and it's on its way to
   your bank account." Caption: "Step 2: confirm"
4. Screen: Payout History. VO: "Track every payout in your payout history."

**End card:** "Next: getting your tenancy agreement signed." (leads into video 12)

---

## 12. Create and sign a tenancy agreement

**Role:** Landlord/tenant · **Length:** ~40s

> Production note: confirm before shooting whether the live flow is native
> in-app AI-drafted agreement + in-app signature, an uploaded document with
> OCR, or both — script beats below assume the AI-draft + in-app-sign path;
> adjust to match whichever is actually live.

**Hook:** "Drafting a tenancy agreement from scratch, or chasing a printed copy
for signatures? Do it all in the app."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: lease page, tap "Tenancy Agreement." VO: "From the lease, open
   Tenancy Agreement." Caption: "Step 1: open the agreement"
2. Screen: choose "Generate with AI" or "Upload existing." VO: "Generate a
   draft, or upload one you already have." Caption: "Step 2: draft or upload"
3. Screen: review/edit draft. VO: "Review the terms and edit anything you
   need." Caption: "Step 3: review"
4. Screen: sign in-app, send to tenant. VO: "Sign it yourself, then send it to
   your tenant to sign on their end." Caption: "Step 4: sign and send"

**End card:** "New tenants often need a guarantor too. Here's that flow."
(leads into video 13)

## 13. Add a guarantor and emergency contacts

**Role:** Landlord/tenant · **Length:** ~25s

**Hook:** "Need a guarantor on file? Send a link, they fill it in themselves."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: tenant profile, tap "Guarantor." VO: "From the tenant's profile,
   open Guarantor." Caption: "Step 1: open Guarantor"
2. Screen: send invite link. VO: "Send them a link instead of collecting the
   details yourself." Caption: "Step 2: send the invite"
3. Screen: filled-in guarantor + emergency contact details appear. VO: "Once
   they fill it in, it's saved to the tenant's file automatically."

**End card:** "If you work with agents, here's how to bring one on." (leads
into video 14)

---

## 14. Invite an agent and set permissions

**Role:** Landlord · **Length:** ~35s

**Hook:** "Want help managing your properties without handing over full
access? Set exactly what your agent can and can't do."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: dashboard, "Invite Agent." VO: "From your dashboard, tap Invite
   Agent." Caption: "Step 1: start the invite"
2. Screen: enter agent's details, assign property. VO: "Enter their details
   and pick which property they'll manage." Caption: "Step 2: assign a
   property"
3. Screen: permission toggles (add tenant, record payment, renew lease,
   upload agreements, manage maintenance, view payments, view reports, remove
   tenant). VO: "Toggle on exactly what they're allowed to do, nothing more."
   Caption: "Step 3: set permissions"
4. Screen: invite sent. VO: "Send the invite. They accept, and get access to
   only what you've allowed."

**End card:** "Next: handling a maintenance request." (leads into video 15)

---

## 15. Submit a maintenance request (tenant)

**Role:** Tenant · **Length:** ~25s

**Hook:** "Something's broken and you don't want to keep calling your
landlord? Report it once, with photos, and track it."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: tenant home, "Maintenance." VO: "Tap Maintenance from your home
   screen." Caption: "Step 1: open Maintenance"
2. Screen: new request, description + priority + photos. VO: "Describe the
   issue, set how urgent it is, and add photos." Caption: "Step 2: describe
   the issue"
3. Screen: submitted, status shown. VO: "Submit, and track the status right
   there until it's resolved."

**End card:** "Here's the landlord side of that same request." (leads into
video 16)

---

## 16. Manage maintenance requests (landlord/agent)

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~25s

**Hook:** "A tenant just reported a problem. Here's how you see it and act on
it."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: Maintenance list. VO: "Open Maintenance to see every request
   across your properties." Caption: "Step 1: open the list"
2. Screen: open one, update status (in progress, resolved). VO: "Open a
   request, update its status as you work on it." Caption: "Step 2: update
   status"
3. Screen: resolved, tenant notified. VO: "Mark it resolved, and your tenant
   gets notified automatically."

**End card:** "Next batch: chat, the AI assistant, reports, and more." (series
bumper)
