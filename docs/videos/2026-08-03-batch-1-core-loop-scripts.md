# Batch 1 pilot scripts: core loop

Pilot batch from [the video series plan](../../.claude/plans/what-if-we-require-smooth-lovelace.md)
(also mirrored here for the repo). Screen recording + voiceover format, vertical
9:16, 30 to 60 seconds each. Cold open on a pain point, cut to the real app flow,
close on one clear next action. Plain, concrete English throughout: no hype
words, no emoji, no invented claims.

Each script has: hook, beats (screen action, voiceover, on-screen caption), and
an end card.

---

## 1. Sign up and get started

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook (VO + caption):** "Managing your rental properties from a notebook,
spreadsheets, and a dozen WhatsApp chats? There's a faster way to start."

Beats:\*\*\*\*

1. Screen: open app, tap "Get started." VO: "Download Property360 and choose
   your role, landlord or agent." Caption: "Step 1: choose your role"
2. Screen: fill name, phone, email. VO: "Enter your details." Caption: "Step 2:
   your details"
3. Screen: email OTP entry. VO: "Confirm your email with the code we send you."
   Caption: "Step 3: confirm your email"
4. Screen: role onboarding carousel finishing at dashboard. VO: "And you're in.
   Your dashboard is ready."

**End card:** "Set up your first property next." (leads into video 2)

---

## 2. Add your first property

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~45s

**Hook:** "Still keeping your property details in a notebook or scattered
across WhatsApp messages? Get it all in one place in under two minutes."

**Beats:**

1. Screen: Dashboard, tap "Add Property" quick action. VO: "From your
   dashboard, tap Add Property." Caption: "Step 1: start the wizard"
2. Screen: property details (name, address, type). VO: "Enter the property
   name and address." Caption: "Step 2: property details"
3. Screen: units + fees. VO: "Add each unit and set the rent and fees, like
   security deposit or agency fee." Caption: "Step 3: units and fees"
4. Screen: amenities. VO: "Pick the amenities that apply." Caption: "Step 4:
   amenities"
5. Screen: photos upload. VO: "Add a few photos." Caption: "Step 5: photos"
6. Screen: review and submit, land on property detail page. VO: "Review and
   save. Your property's live in the app."

**End card:** "Now add a tenant to a unit." (leads into video 3)

---

## 3. Add a tenant

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~45s

**Hook:** "Onboarding a new tenant usually means paperwork and back and forth.
Here it's one flow, and they get an invite straight to their phone."

**Beats:**

1. Screen: property, select vacant unit, tap "Add Tenant." VO: "Open the
   property, pick the vacant unit, and tap Add Tenant." Caption: "Step 1:
   pick the unit"
2. Screen: tenant details (name, phone, email). VO: "Enter the tenant's
   details." Caption: "Step 2: tenant details"
3. Screen: lease terms and fees. VO: "Set the lease term, rent, and any other
   fees." Caption: "Step 3: lease terms and fees"
4. Screen: review and send invite. VO: "Review, then send. Your tenant gets an
   invite to accept and set up their own account."

**End card:** "Next: send your first invoice." (leads into video 4)

---

## 4. Create and send an invoice

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook:** "Chasing rent with phone calls and reminder texts every month?
Send an invoice instead, and let the app do the reminding."

**Beats:**

1. Screen: tenant/lease page, tap "Create Invoice." VO: "From the tenant's
   lease, tap Create Invoice." Caption: "Step 1: start an invoice"
2. Screen: line items, tax, due date. VO: "Add the line items, rent, service
   charge, whatever applies, and set the due date." Caption: "Step 2: line
   items and due date"
3. Screen: send. VO: "Send it. Your tenant gets it instantly, and can pay part
   or all of it." Caption: "Sent"

**End card:** "See how a manual payment gets recorded next." (leads into video 5)

---

## 5. Record a manual payment

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook:** "Tenant paid you cash, or sent a bank transfer straight to your
account? Log it in seconds, no spreadsheet needed."

**Beats:**

1. Screen: tenant's profile, tap "Record Payment." VO: "Open the tenant's
   profile and tap Record Payment." Caption: "Step 1: open Record Payment"
2. Screen: amount, date, method (cash/transfer/cheque). VO: "Enter the amount,
   date, and how they paid." Caption: "Step 2: amount and method"
3. Screen: save, show updated payment history. VO: "Save, and it's reflected
   in your reports and their payment history right away."

**End card:** "Here's what it looks like from the tenant's side, paying
through the app." (leads into video 6)

---

## 6. Pay rent through the app (tenant)

**Role:** Tenant · **Length:** ~30s

**Hook:** "No more queuing at the bank, or sending a transfer and hoping your
landlord confirms it. Pay your rent from your phone."

**Beats:**

1. Screen: tenant home, see amount due. VO: "Your rent due shows right on your
   home screen." Caption: "See what's due"
2. Screen: tap "Pay," choose card/bank transfer/USSD. VO: "Tap Pay, and choose
   card, bank transfer, or USSD." Caption: "Choose how to pay"
3. Screen: complete Paystack checkout, confirmation. VO: "Confirm, and you're
   done. Your landlord's notified instantly."

**End card:** "Your receipt is saved automatically. Here's where to find it."
(leads into video 7)

---

## 7. Find your payment history and receipts

**Role:** Landlord/agent/tenant · **Length:** ~25s

**Hook:** "Landlord, or your accountant, asking for proof rent was paid last
month? It's already in the app."

**Beats:**

1. Screen: navigate to Payment History. VO: "Go to Payment History from your
   dashboard." Caption: "Step 1: open Payment History"
2. Screen: filter/search a specific payment. VO: "Find any payment by tenant,
   date, or property." Caption: "Step 2: find the payment"
3. Screen: open receipt, download/share. VO: "Open it, and download or share
   the receipt directly." Caption: "Step 3: download or share"

**End card:** "Lease ending soon? Here's how renewal works." (leads into video 8)

---

## 8. Renew a lease

**Role:** Landlord/agent · **Length:** ~25s

**Hook:** "Lease about to expire? Renewing takes two taps, not a new contract
from scratch."

**Beats:**

1. Screen: tenant profile, tap "Renew Lease." VO: "Open the tenant's profile
   and tap Renew Lease." Caption: "Step 1: open Renew Lease"
2. Screen: adjust rent/duration if needed. VO: "Adjust the rent or lease
   length if anything's changed." Caption: "Step 2: update the terms"
3. Screen: confirm, updated lease dates shown. VO: "Confirm, and the lease is
   extended. Nothing else to redo."

**End card:** "That's the core loop. More how-tos coming." (series bumper)

## Notes for whoever shoots these

- Use a real staging or production account with realistic sample data (a real
  property name, a real-looking tenant), not empty placeholder states.
- Keep each screen action on-screen for at least 1.5 to 2 seconds so it reads
  clearly even sped up in editing.
- Reuse the same intro bumper and end-card style across all eight so the batch
  reads as one series before extending to Batches 2 to 4.
- Verify the tenancy agreement flow before scripting Batch 2's version of that
  video (see plan: confirm native in-app signing vs. DocuSeal is what's live).
