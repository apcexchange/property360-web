# Batch 4 scripts: edge and setup features

Continues [Batch 3](./2026-08-03-batch-3-engagement-scripts.md). Same format:
vertical 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds (these are smaller/quicker features), screen
recording + voiceover, plain concrete English, no hype, no emoji, no invented
claims.

---

## 27. Reset a forgotten password

**Role:** All roles · **Length:** ~15s

**Hook:** "Forgot your password? You're back in in under a minute."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: login screen, "Forgot password." VO: "Tap Forgot Password on the
   login screen." Caption: "Step 1: tap Forgot Password"
2. Screen: enter email/phone, receive OTP. VO: "Enter your email, and confirm
   the code we send you." Caption: "Step 2: confirm the code"
3. Screen: set new password. VO: "Set a new password, and you're back in."

**End card:** "Want to skip typing your password next time? Turn on Face ID."
(leads into video 28)

## 28. Turn on Face ID or fingerprint login

   to turn it on." Caption: "Step 2: confirm once"

   your face or fingerprint."

**End card:** "On the more serious side: here's how a quit notice works."

## 29. Issue a quit notice

**Role:** Landlord · **Length:** ~25s

**Hook:** "Need to formally ask a tenant to vacate? Do it properly, in the
app, not a text message."

**Beats:**
1. Screen: tenant profile, "Issue Quit Notice." VO: "From the tenant's
   profile, tap Issue Quit Notice." Caption: "Step 1: open Quit Notice"
2. Screen: select grounds, review notice. VO: "Select the grounds, and review
   the notice before sending." Caption: "Step 2: select grounds"
3. Screen: served, tenant notified. VO: "Serve it, and your tenant sees it in
   their app, with the details on record."

**End card:** "Not signed up yet? Here's what you can see before you even
create an account." (leads into video 30)
